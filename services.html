General repair and maintenance

Brakes

Pads, rotors, calipers, lines and hydraulics. If you're hearing grinding or feeling the pedal go soft, don't wait on it.

Air conditioning

Diagnosis, recharge, compressor and component replacement. Texas summers don't leave much room to put this off.

Electrical diagnostics

Check engine lights, no-starts, charging problems, sensors and wiring. We chase the actual fault instead of throwing parts at it.

Tune-ups & maintenance

Plugs, coils, filters, fluids and scheduled service. The cheapest way to keep a transmission healthy is to keep the rest of the truck healthy.

Transmission service

Fluid and filter service on a healthy unit. Not a fix for a slipping transmission, but it's what keeps a good one from becoming a rebuild.

About the shop

20+ years on Irving Blvd

Arevalo's Auto Repair is an independent, family-run shop in Irving, Texas. We've spent two decades building a business on referrals, which only works if you quote honestly, do the work right and stand behind it.

Over the years the work concentrated around transmissions — mostly Ford, Chevy and Ram trucks — because that's the job other shops send away and the one we got good at. We rebuild in-house, we quote before we start, and we warranty what we build for six months.
