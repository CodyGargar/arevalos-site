Know before you drive it

Signs your transmission is going out

Driving on a slipping transmission turns a rebuild into a replacement. If you notice any of these, stop by or call.

Free diagnostic — call now
  • Slipping or revving between gears
  • Hard, banging or delayed shifts
  • Won't move in drive or reverse
  • Red fluid spots under the truck
  • Burnt smell after driving
  • Whining, humming or grinding
  • Check engine or wrench light on
  • Shifts into limp mode on the highway

How it works

From your driveway to back on the road

Call us and describe it

Tell us the year, make, model and what it's doing. We'll give you a ballpark over the phone before you spend a dollar. If it can't drive, we'll arrange the tow.

Free diagnostic

We scan the codes, check fluid and pressures, and road test it if it's driveable. Sometimes it's a solenoid or a sensor, not a rebuild — and we'll tell you that.

Written quote, then your call

You get the price before we start. No work happens until you say go, and the number doesn't change unless we find something and call you first.

Rebuilt in-house

The unit comes out, gets torn down, cleaned and inspected. Worn clutches, seals, bands and hard parts get replaced, then it goes back in and gets road tested.

6-month warranty

Parts and labor on the rebuild. If something isn't right, bring it back to us and we'll take care of it.
