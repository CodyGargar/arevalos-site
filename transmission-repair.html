Rebuild vs. replace

What a rebuild actually means

A lot of shops quote you a "transmission job" and drop in a used unit out of a wrecked truck. You're buying somebody else's wear with no idea how many miles are on it.

A rebuild is different. We pull your unit, take it apart completely, and clean and inspect every piece. Clutches, seals, gaskets, bands, filters and any worn hard parts get replaced. It goes back together to spec, back in the truck, and out on a road test before you get the keys.

It costs more up front than a junkyard swap and it lasts a lot longer. That's the whole trade.

Symptoms

Bring it in before it strands you

Most transmissions give warning for weeks before they quit. Caught early, it's often a solenoid, a sensor or a fluid problem instead of a full rebuild.

Free diagnostic — call now
  • Slipping or revving between gears
  • Hard, banging or delayed shifts
  • No movement in drive or reverse
  • Red fluid on the driveway
  • Burnt smell after a drive
  • Whining, humming or grinding
  • Shudder at highway speed
  • Limp mode / stuck in one gear
  • Check engine or wrench light
  • Gear indicator flashing

Questions

Straight answers

How much does a transmission rebuild cost?

Rebuilds start at $1,400. The final number depends on the unit, the vehicle and what we find once it's apart. Call or email your year, make and model and we'll quote it.

Is the diagnostic really free?

Yes. We pull codes, check fluid and pressures and road test it if it's driveable, at no charge. You'll know what you're dealing with before you spend anything.

How long does it take?

It depends on the unit and parts availability. We'll give you a realistic timeline when we quote the job, and we'll tell you if it's a work truck priority. Call for current turnaround.

Do you tow?

Towing is free within 15 miles of the shop when we do the major work on the vehicle. Outside that radius, call us and we'll work something out.

What does the warranty cover?

Six months on parts and labor for the rebuild we performed. Bring it back to us and we'll take care of it.

Do you rebuild, or install used units?

We rebuild in-house. Your unit comes out, gets torn down, cleaned, inspected and reassembled with new wear parts. We're not putting a junkyard transmission in your truck.

Do you work on anything besides Ford, Chevy and Ram?

Those are our focus and where our experience is deepest, but we take other makes case by case. Call and ask — we'll tell you honestly if it's a job for us.
